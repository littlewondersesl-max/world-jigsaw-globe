Hints

Tap the stack to draw a piece.

Tap or drag the stack (bottom-left) to draw a piece, then drop it on the globe or into a drawer. Pull the faint wooden handles at the screen edges to open the drawers. Turn a piece with ⟲ ⟳, the mouse wheel, or Q / E keys. Drag the globe to spin it. Drop a piece on a top-down conveyor (right edge) for size, heading, or a Finderator meter — costs coins. Pieces pushed off float in zero-G; throw hard to orbit.
